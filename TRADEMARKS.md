# PHOS trademark guidance (draft)

The Apache License, Version 2.0 does not grant general permission to use a
licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names. The PHOS
name and any PHOS logo are distinct from the copyright license.

Forks, derived products, and redistributions must not imply official PHOS
endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or affiliation. Descriptive references
to PHOS—for example, to identify the origin of an unmodified fork—remain
permitted where legally appropriate.

This draft does not assert that PHOS is a registered trademark or that any
person or organization has exclusive trademark ownership. Maintainers should
obtain legal review and publish verified ownership and logo-use terms before
relying on this document as a final trademark policy.
